Every skin can do all of this

The look is different. The features are the same.

  • 📡

    Connect your radio

    Pair over Bluetooth or plug in with USB.

  • 💬

    Messages

    Channels and direct messages, with unread badges and delivery status.

  • 🌦️

    Weather

    Sends your ZIP code to your mesh weather bot and shows the reply as a card.

  • 🗺️

    Network

    See nearby companions, repeaters, and rooms. Send adverts. Tap a node to message it.

  • 🪪

    Contact cards

    Share and add contacts using the MeshCore app's link format.

  • 📍

    Map

    See contacts that share a GPS position on an OpenStreetMap map, cached for offline use.

  • 📶

    Works offline

    Install it once and it keeps working with no internet.

  • 🔓

    Private

    No account, no server. Your messages and settings stay on your device.

Get started in five steps

  1. Open a skin. Tap any Launch button on this page.
  2. Enter your ZIP code or town. Use whatever your weather bot understands, such as 30301 or Atlanta, GA.
  3. Enter your weather bot's channel. The default is #weather. The app adds it to your radio for you.
  4. Tap Connect and pick your radio.
  5. Open the Weather tab and tap Get weather.

Add it to your phone's home screen

iPhone / iPad (Safari): tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.

Android (Chrome): tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.

Install on a computer

In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.

Bluetooth and USB radio connections need Chrome, Edge, or another Chromium browser.

Try more than one

Each skin is its own app with its own settings. You can install all three side by side.
